## Educational principles

Overview-to-body navigation provides a route between system context and individual objects. The recording does not demonstrate flight-plan consequences or explain the model assumptions in depth.

## 3D / visualization principles

Textured body views and orbit paths make object identity and location legible. Relative distances and size cannot be inferred as physically to scale.
