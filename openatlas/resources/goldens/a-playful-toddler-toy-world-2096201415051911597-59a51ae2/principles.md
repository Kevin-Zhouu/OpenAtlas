## Why this works

Open walls and elevated camera keep floor objects and large play equipment visible. Warm restrained palette and compact peripheral controls create a coherent miniature scene.

## Visual principles

Use open-sided isometric rooms to expose spatial activity zones.

## Interaction principles

Place contextual labels near the active object.

## Educational principles

Different toy areas offer a potential spatial context for activity-based learning. Character play and changing activities are not tied to explanation, prediction or a learning goal.

## 3D / visualization principles

Open walls and elevated camera keep floor objects and large play equipment visible. Furniture and toys are attractive but primarily illustrative.

## Implementation patterns worth studying

Creator prompt requests Three.js and Vite; those are stated intentions, not verified source. No original repository was found.

## Weaknesses

Primarily a presentation and activity reference, not demonstrated educational content.

## Anti-patterns

Do not treat cute animation as evidence of child learning. Do not attribute autonomous motion to meaningful user control.

## What OpenAtlas should learn

- Use open-sided isometric rooms to expose spatial activity zones.
- Place contextual labels near the active object.
- Hide secondary activity choices until requested.

## What OpenAtlas should NOT copy

- Do not treat cute animation as evidence of child learning.
- Do not attribute autonomous motion to meaningful user control.
