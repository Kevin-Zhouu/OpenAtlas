## Why this works

A confined volume makes above-water weather and surface motion visible together. A single dominant cube and isolated dial make the main control easy to identify.

## Visual principles

Make the changed parameter and visible consequence share a viewport.

## Interaction principles

Use a bounded scene to keep several coupled effects readable.

## Educational principles

One changing control is paired with conspicuous changes in sea state and rain. A single bundled intensity value hides independent causes and cannot teach meteorology alone.

## 3D / visualization principles

A confined volume makes above-water weather and surface motion visible together. Transparent cube boundaries are an artistic container, not a physical atmospheric boundary.

## Implementation patterns worth studying

Creator names Three.js and states that the demo was recorded and edited. No original source was available to examine wave or weather equations.

## Weaknesses

Powerful cause/effect presentation, but physical calibration and explanatory teaching are limited.

## Anti-patterns

Do not copy an arbitrary weather-intensity mapping as scientific law. Do not equate spectacular fluid rendering with a teaching explanation.
