## Why this works

Different representational tasks receive separate views, reducing the need to put the whole plant and every flow relationship into one diagram.

## Visual principles

Use one color vocabulary for water, steam and fuel throughout the exhibit.

## Interaction principles

Make visibility, cuts and disassembly reversible. Preserve play/pause state predictably when changing modes.

## Educational principles

Connect the architecture view to a simplified circuit, then explicitly explain where that circuit abstracts the real system.

## 3D / visualization principles

Layer separation makes hidden structure visible. A circuit diagram can be clearer than full 3D for following a flow; keep that distinction.

## Implementation patterns worth studying

A shared state for visibility and playback is a useful pattern inferred from observed behavior. No original source is available to confirm the implementation.

## Weaknesses

Explanations name components more often than explain why they are necessary. Initial desktop geometry is partly below the fold.

## Anti-patterns

Physical-looking motion without an explicit time/scale disclaimer; revealing every control before establishing the main learning question.

## What OpenAtlas should learn

Combine linked explanatory representations with reversible inspection and source-aware captions.

## What OpenAtlas should NOT copy

Do not reproduce the original layout verbatim or treat simplified geometry as an engineering specification.
