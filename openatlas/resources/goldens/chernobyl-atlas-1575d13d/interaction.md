# Interaction Analysis

## Primary interaction
Initial: The power-block view is assembled at 0%.
Action: Press End on the Disassembly slider.
Response: Status reaches 100%, structures separate and previously occluded labels become visible (interaction-1.png).
Why it matters: The relationship of architectural shell, shielding, graphite and fuel becomes inspectable.

## Secondary interactions
Steam Circuit replaces the power block with a numbered reactor–separator–turbine–condenser–feedwater representation (interaction-2.png). Pause changes the status to PAUSED and the control to Play motion (interaction-3.png). Reactor in Motion changes the spatial representation while preserving paused state (interaction-4.png). Section cut Home changes Off to 0% and removes enclosing geometry (interaction-5.png). About & sources opens the interpretation and references dialog (interaction-6.png). Reset all controls restores full visibility and playback (interaction-7.png).

## Educational consequence
The learner can distinguish the plant architecture from a simplified direct steam cycle. The explanatory footer warns that motion represents direction rather than operating speed. These are meaningful inspection operations; no reactor-physics parameter experiment was observed.

## Capture and responsive limitations
Regular Chrome required the site's ordinary ChatGPT sign-in. No credentials or authentication state were exported. Desktop viewport was 1440×900; the document extends to about 1260px, leaving some geometry and controls below the initial fold. At 390×844 the reactor stage remains visible and controls continue below it. No browser errors were observed in the queried error log. Seven interaction captures retain one extra state to document reset and viewport behavior.
