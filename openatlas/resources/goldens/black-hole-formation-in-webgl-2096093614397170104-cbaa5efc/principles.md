## Why this works

A persistent stage and object-focused composition support a chronological explanation without requiring a long article.

## Visual principles

Reserve the dominant area for the phenomenon; align brief text consistently and keep the chapter rail secondary.

## Interaction principles

Chapter selection should update text and scene together. Add learner-controlled comparisons when teaching causal relationships.

## Educational principles

Separate supported equilibrium, fuel exhaustion, collapse and horizon; qualify alternate outcomes and illustrative timing.

## 3D / visualization principles

Show changes in radius and surrounding material continuously; distinguish a horizon from a luminous accretion image.

## Implementation patterns worth studying

A coordinated chapter-state controller would be useful, but no source was available to verify the actual implementation.

## Weaknesses

The video shows a guided presentation, not an experiment. Correctness and mobile behavior remain partly unverified.

## Anti-patterns

Mistaking cinematic realism for a validated physical simulation.

## What OpenAtlas should learn

Use concise staged visual storytelling as an introduction, then expose a parameter that changes an outcome.

## What OpenAtlas should NOT copy

The precise visual identity, or the assumption that chapter navigation earns top interaction scores.
