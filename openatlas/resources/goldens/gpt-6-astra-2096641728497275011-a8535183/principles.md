# Mini World — provisional visual principles

## Why this works
A small spherical world places several visible environments in one bounded scene.

## Visual principles
Large region icons and consistent foreground panels leave the terrain central.

## Interaction principles
Region and zoom affordances are visible. Their effectiveness is unverified.

## Educational principles
This could support vocabulary or environmental comparison, but no such learning was observed.

## 3D / visualization principles
Overview and close-up stills suggest a useful whole-to-region composition; temporal continuity is unknown.

## Implementation patterns worth studying
No verified source. Do not infer a camera or movement architecture from still images.

## Weaknesses
No temporal evidence or working deployment.

## Anti-patterns
Assuming advertised child-friendly controls are usable by children without observation.

## What OpenAtlas should learn
Study the bounded-world composition and low reading burden of region buttons as design hypotheses.

## What OpenAtlas should NOT copy
Do not copy the art or claim that collecting stars establishes learning.
