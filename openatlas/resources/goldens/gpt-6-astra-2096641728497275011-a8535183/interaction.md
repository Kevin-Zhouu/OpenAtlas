# Interaction evidence limitation

Four original screenshots show Snowflake Peak, Sunshine Sands, Blueberry Bay and Whispering Woods, with forest/desert/ocean/snow region controls, a character and zoom affordances. These are distinct static states, not an observed action-response sequence.

The creator post describes movement, zooming and swimming and supplies a recreation prompt. Those are claims and requirements, not tested interactions. Neither the visible original thread nor focused search established a live URL or original repository. Do not treat the prompt as executable evidence.

No temporal, mobile, console or learning-outcome evaluation is possible from this evidence package. The four stills and their public-catalogue checksums are retained in reference/stills.json. Hero is a PNG conversion of the first still, not a browser capture.
